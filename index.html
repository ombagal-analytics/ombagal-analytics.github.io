Simple rule: 50% before I start, 50% when I deliver. UPI or bank transfer only.

Accepted Payment Methods

  • UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) — preferred method.
  • Direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) — details shared on request.

Refund Policy

  • Advance payment (50%) is non-refundable once work has begun.

  • No refund is applicable once the final dashboard has been delivered.

Retainer Cancellation

  • A 2-day notice is required to pause or cancel a monthly retainer.
  • No partial refunds for mid-month cancellations.

📋 Terms & Conditions

By hiring Om Bagal Analytics, you agree to these terms. They protect both of us.

Scope of Work

  • Each project scope is agreed upon before work begins via WhatsApp.

Revisions
